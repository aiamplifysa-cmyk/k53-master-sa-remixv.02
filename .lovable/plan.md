# K53 Master SA — Learner & Driver Training App

A South African K53 study app based on your uploaded starter site, questions, road signs and the official K53 Code B / Code EB practical test manuals.

## Pages
- **Home** (public): what the app offers, sign-up / sign-in buttons.
- **Sign in / Sign up**: email and password accounts.
- **Dashboard**: progress overview — tests taken, best score, weak topics, signs studied.
- **Road Signs**: searchable, filterable sign cards (regulatory, warning, guidance) from your signs file, with a flashcard mode.
- **Practice Questions**: study by topic (rules of the road, signs, vehicle controls) with instant feedback and explanations.
- **Mock Test**: timed learner's test in the official format with pass/fail marks per section, and a results review.
- **Practical Test Guide (K53 Code B & EB)**: the manual modules (yard test, road test, gear changing, lane changing, following distance, etc.) as readable step-by-step checklists, plus downloadable original PDFs.
- **DLTC Guide**: what to bring and how booking works.

## Saved per learner
- Every mock test result and answers.
- Which signs and practice questions were answered right or wrong, to power "weak topics" and "retry missed questions".

## Design
Fresh, distinctive look inspired by the SA flag green from your starter (not a copy of the generic template), mobile friendly, large tap targets.

## Notes
- Uses your 373-question bank (original 100, Tests A/B/C, motorcycle, light and heavy vehicle) with its sign images. Questions marked as having missing or conflicting answers are hidden from tests and listed for you to fix.
- Learners pick their licence code (Code 1 motorcycle, Code 2 light, Code 3 heavy) and get matching questions and practical manuals (motorcycle, light, heavy, Code EB).
- Road sign charts, signage catalogue, vehicle controls manual and DLTC guide are added to the guides as readable sections plus downloadable PDFs.
- Mock test section sizes and pass marks will follow the standard learner's test (Code 1/2/3 style) — confirm if you want different numbers.

## Technical details
- Lovable Cloud for accounts, profiles (display name, licence code chosen), test attempts and answer history, all private per user.
- Questions, signs and manual modules shipped as app data; PDFs uploaded as hosted files.
- Protected pages behind sign-in; Home and guides public.
